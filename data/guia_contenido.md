CHANNEL STYLE GUIDE (edit freely; the scriptwriter reads this every time)
- Voice: confident, friendly, slightly witty. Explain like you're talking to a friend who loves GTA.
- Always separate what's confirmed, reported, rumor and opinion. Saying "we don't know yet" is fine.
- Give players something useful: what it means for them, what to watch for, what to ignore.
- Context beats hype: compare with GTA V, Red Dead Redemption 2, or industry history when the sources allow it.
- Never claim to have played the game unless the channel has its own footage of it.
- Avoid: "INSANE", "you won't believe", fake urgency, all-caps titles, misleading thumbnails.
